    Paper trading only — no real orders are placed. This runs continuously on the Spring Boot server, so it keeps checking prices and managing any open trade even while your browser or laptop is closed. Signals come from a simple SMA/RSI/MACD/Bollinger heuristic on Yahoo Finance data — for learning, not investment advice. Averaging in (buying more if price drops) is capped by "Max buy legs" as a safety limit, but it is still a real trading risk: a stock that keeps falling can still produce a loss even after averaging. Always verify prices on your broker's terminal before acting on anything shown here.
